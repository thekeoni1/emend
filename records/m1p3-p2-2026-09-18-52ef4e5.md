## Finding P2-F1: `values` and `empty`

Measured `values=2089191` where specs/0010 says
2089138, and `empty=5410` where it
says 5463: 53
cells moved from one to the other. The two still sum to the cells
total, which both routes and the spec agree on, so no cell is lost;
the partition line sits elsewhere than the spec's figures put it.

The cause is measured, not guessed. Exactly 5410 cells hold
neither a `<v>` nor an `<f>`, and zero cells hold neither while
holding any child element at all, so the 53 further cells the spec's
`empty` figure adds are not in this corpus. The reader is not
changed and the run is not repeated until it agrees: specs/0001 says
a departure is a finding to record and planning rules which side is
wrong.

specs/0010 also warns that `numbers_parsed` may fall short of
`number` where a cached value is not a number this layer reads. It
does not: the whole shortfall is the 5410 Number cells that
hold no value, every one of the remaining
