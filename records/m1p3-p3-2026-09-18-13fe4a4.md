## Finding P2-F1, as it stands now

P2's run measured `values` 2089191 and
`empty` 5410 where the spec then said
2089138 and 5463, recorded the difference as finding P2-F1 and did
not change the reader. Planning resolved it against itself: the
spec's two figures came from a survey regex that missed 53 `<v>`
elements carrying an attribute, planning's own reader and the crate
agreed, and ruling 1 corrected the spec. This run measures the
corrected figures, so the table above has no departure in it.

The measurement that settled it was made again here: exactly
5410 cells hold neither a `<v>` nor an
`<f>`, and zero cells hold neither while holding any child element
at all.
