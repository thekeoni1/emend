# M3 part 1: what streams and what is held, measured (the decision record)

`specs/0023-scale.md`, "P3", with P2 verification rulings 1 to 7. This
record names what the scale runs measured, each finding's kind, and what
the later parts of Milestone 3 design around. Workbooks are cited by tier
letter and id only (`records/m3p1-gen-2026-10-01-a509ad3.md`); no
generated content appears here.

Primaries:

- `records/logs/m3p1-p2/run.log`: the 192 sample runs at 8d05845 (24
  samples, tiers a, b, d and c, labels host and i686, a 2048 MiB address
  space cap and a 3600 s timeout on both), the derivations and the 32-bit
  control. The 96 record pairs `records/m3p1-scale-<tier>-<sample>-2026-10-01-8d05845`.
- `records/logs/m3p1-p3/run.log`: the three read runs of P2 verification
  ruling 7 at 6578925, the names run's two stderr files printed, part 4d's
  two samples rerun, and the open record.
- `records/logs/m3p1-p3/size.log` and `records/size-m3p1-2026-10-02-6578925.md`.

## Tier c's outcomes (P2 verification rulings 5 and 6)

- ok on both labels: edit-formula, delete-rows, insert-columns,
  delete-columns, props, protect, sheet, remove. Five of these rewrite the
  zip64 sheet entry on i686 under the cap: edit-formula, delete-rows,
  insert-columns, delete-columns and protect, the largest peak 7860 kB
  (edit-formula on the host; P2 verification ruling 6 says 7864 kB, which
  is remove's, a raw copy),
  the entry's uncompressed sizes printed whole (5025024826 in; 4985617758
  to 5026073402 out) with the planted 32-bit truncation caught. The other
  three copy it raw.
- Refused with "a row or column index or count is out of range": 14
  samples, 7 at the recording call (format, validate, cf, scale, table,
  comment, image) and 7 at the write through 8d05845's check (edit,
  style, font, border, date, formula, link). Each adds a row after the
  sheet's last, which is the grid's last on tier c.
- Refused with "a row or column would be moved off the worksheet":
  insert-rows, at the shift.
- names: killed on both labels by its own second write (P2 verification
  ruling 1, class "holds in the tool"). The stderr files, as P3's run.log
  prints them: on the host "memory allocation of 2198929408 bytes
  failed" (signal 6, peak 1078036 kB); on i686 "capacity overflow" from
  `alloc/src/raw_vec` (exit 101, peak 1075732 kB).
- The two prediction disagreements, link on both labels: the P1 survey
  listed link's calls without its `set_cell` at `max_row + 1`
  (`tools/emend-copy/src/main.rs`:3034-3038); a survey omission, not a
  defect.

The other write paths (styles, formats, merges, validations, rules,

about 2.5 to 3 times the text itself. `--cells` peaks 96 kB above
`--shared-strings` on the host and the same on i686, so the cell pass
adds nothing measurable beside the table.

## Findings, each with its kind

1. A cell value, clear or style recorded off the grid was written, past
   row 1048576 (P1 finding 3). **Defect, fixed** in 8d05845 (P1
   verification ruling 5); the core's growth for the part is 128 section
   bytes, all of it that commit's (size.log; the catalogue commit adds 0).
2. The shared strings table is held whole by `Package::shared_strings`.
   **Expected**, measured above: about 46 bytes per unique item on i686.
   Under a 2048 MiB address space that is a ceiling near 46 million
   unique strings for the table alone, before anything else the process
   holds; a consumer resolving cells of such a workbook cannot. **Carried
   to part 5** (read-back as typed values): whether a resolver that does
   not hold the table (an index into the part, or resolving on demand)
   is designed, or the limit is stated in the catalogue.
3. An existing comments VML part or drawing part is read whole when a
   comment or an image is added beside it (the P1 survey; no tier holds
   one, so not measured). **Design limit, carried to part 6**.
4. The names, validate, cf, table and comment samples hold the whole
   output in the tool's memory. **Holds in the tool** (P1 verification
   ruling 7), the crate unchanged; on tier c names cannot complete on
   either target. Carried as a note on the harness tool's sample design,
   nothing in the crate.
5. A rewrite's temporaries are files in `TMPDIR`, sized by the part,
   invisible to the address-space cap and to peak RSS; on tier c the
   uncompressed temporary is about the entry's size (the code's reading,
   not measured). A run killed by an abort leaves them: the names host
   abort left 2 files, 955 bytes (P2's run.log). That they are the
   crate's `TempFile`s, whose `Drop` does not run on an abort, is the
   code's reading (P2 verification ruling 2). **Design limit, stated in
   the catalogue**; the runner's `leftover` column (the run directory
   only) **carried to part 7**.
6. The host xlsx-diff ran for both labels on tier c (decision 4); its
   sha256 1445523df9522bff56d4f0e03d7bde2ab333bac294020da256de42c01ef71f6b
   is printed at line 1047 of P2's run.log. The record script carries no
   binary hash; line 1051's prose there says otherwise and is wrong (P2
   verification ruling 3, which numbers it 1052; the prose is on 1051). **Carried to part 7**: the record script's
   header gains the two hashes.
7. The link prediction (above). **A survey omission**, recorded.

## What the later parts design around

- Part 2 and part 5, the read side: `Package::cells` streams at any size
  measured; a typed read-back that resolves shared strings meets finding
  2's table.
- Part 6, comments and images: finding 3's whole reads.
- Every part: a write that adds a row after the last refuses on a full
  sheet (finding 1), and a rewrite of a large part needs disk in
  `TMPDIR` of about the part's uncompressed size (finding 5).
- Part 7: findings 5 and 6's harness carries.

The claim in `CLAUDE.md` that the core streams where the format allows
it is, for reading cells, rewriting a worksheet and copying untouched
entries, now measured on a 32-bit target over an entry larger than its
address space; the package header's "What streams and what is held"
states it with these figures.
