## The finding

The budget figure, 478992 section bytes, is above the ceiling of
393216 by 85776. This is the size finding of
`specs/0016-new-workbook-and-sheets.md`, "Planning rulings during P4",
rulings 10 and 11. Its cause was measured by
`records/logs/m2p1-p4/size-attribution.log`, which rebuilt the committed
`probe-emend-sheets-write-xml` at 1012208 section bytes and three variants of it:

- (a) no `Package::new` call: 903196, a difference of -109012;
- (b) no sheet edits: 986700, a difference of -25508;
- (c) `Package::new`'s bytes written to a file and reopened through
  `BufReader<File>`: 1012384, a difference of 176.

`Package::new` opens and writes a `Package<Cursor<Vec<u8>>>` inside the
crate, so the read and write paths are compiled a second time beside the
copy over the consumer's own reader, and (c) shows a consumer cannot avoid
it. The maintainer's decision of 2026-09-25 ("Decisions for the
maintainer", 6) commits this record as measured, with the excess a
finding, and leaves the ceiling unchanged until P5's figure exists. The
fix is P5 ("P5: one copy of the read and write paths"), which erases the
reader once and the writer once inside the crate and measures the thirteen
probes again. This record stands as the measurement P5's record is
compared with.

## Reading these figures

File sizes on this target change in whole 4096-byte steps. They are not
themselves whole pages: all thirteen probes leave the same remainder, 2596
bytes, modulo 4096, so it is every DIFFERENCE between them that is a whole
number of pages. A change under a page is therefore invisible in the file
size, while the section totals, from `size -A`, resolve to the byte. Both
are given for every figure above.
