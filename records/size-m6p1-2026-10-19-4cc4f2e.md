The stripped `i686-unknown-linux-musl` sizes of the twenty-nine size
probes, measured by `records/logs/m6p1-p3/size.log` after Milestone 6
part 1's chartsheets (`specs/0038-chartsheets.md`, "Size and
packaging"): a chartsheet's chart read and written, `add_chartsheet`,
the root rule and the chartsheet form, everything under the feature
`charts`, and the charts probe's work grown by `add_chartsheet` of its
column chart and a `charts` read of the new part. The feature

and the charts probe's growth over part 6's record, this part's cost, is
judged against planning's estimate of 6000 to 11000 section bytes, a
finding above 15000. Each other probe's growth over M5 part 6's record is
judged in its band: the core, the format probe over the same probe
rebuilt at that record's commit, expected 0 and accepted up to 2000
section bytes; the images and comments probes expected 0 and accepted up
to 1500 each; every other probe expected 0 and accepted up to 2000; a
growth outside its band, or a
shrink, is a finding, recorded and not fixed. 22 of the twenty-eight
probes other than the charts probe grew or shrank. Each figure that moved is attributed to the commits that
touched that probe's own sources, by part 7's per-file method
(`specs/0030`, decision 2, A). The budget is the core probe,
`probe-emend-format-write-xml`, over `probe-zip-xml`, against the
ceiling Milestone 2 set and Milestones 3 to 6 keep
(`specs/0017-fuller-styles.md`, decision 1, A). This record follows
`records/size-m5p6-2026-10-18-ef9da37.md`, M5 part 6's, which stands as history.

## Reading these figures

File sizes on this target change in whole 4096-byte steps. They are not
themselves whole pages: all the probes leave the same remainder, 2596
bytes, modulo 4096, so it is every DIFFERENCE between them that is a whole
number of pages. A change under a page is therefore invisible in the file
size, while the section totals, from `size -A`, resolve to the byte. Both
are given for every figure above.
